About Khawar Riaz Salon

A sanctuary of refined grooming, signature styling, and individual focus on MM Alam Road, Lahore.

Excellence in Execution

The Khawar Riaz Vision

Khawar Riaz Salon was established with a clear mandate: to redefine personal care in Lahore by delivering world-class hair styling, restorative skincare, and grooming services tailored specifically for both men and women.

Khawar Riaz serves as the creative name behind the salon, emphasizing professional craftsmanship, thorough hair analysis, and individualized beauty consultations. Rather than relying on temporary trends, our approach centers on accentuating each client's unique features, facial geometry, and lifestyle needs.

Located on the 2nd Floor of 90-B1 MM Alam Road (above KFC) in Gulberg III, our space is engineered to provide an atmosphere of relaxation, cleanliness, and discrete luxury.
