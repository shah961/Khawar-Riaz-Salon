Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Khawar Riaz Salon for Men & Women, accessible from khawarriazsalon.com, we respect your privacy. When you request an appointment or communicate with us through our contact form, email, phone, or WhatsApp, we may collect information including your name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment date, and service details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Process and confirm your appointment reservations.
  • Communicate regarding scheduling, timing adjustments, or salon inquiries.
  • Improve our service delivery and client management.
  • Comply with applicable local regulations in Pakistan.

3. Third-Party Communications & Links

Our website provides direct links to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp (wa.me) and Google Maps for your convenience. Please note that third-party services operate under their own privacy policies, and Khawar Riaz Salon is not responsible for data managed by external services once you navigate away from our site.

4. Cookies & Web Analytics

This static website does not store invasive tracking cookies. General, non-identifying technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and page requests) may be recorded by our hosting platform for performance monitoring and security purposes.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate technical and operational measures to safeguard client personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We retain appointment communication logs only as long as required to maintain client service records.
